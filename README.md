# Dimensional

A 3D co-op browser game for **2–20 players**. Each player is stuck in a different
dimension (grassy, upside-down, icy, floating, flat 2D, dark, underwater…), but
everyone can still see each other. Nobody can win alone.

Play: https://henrythebaby1-ops.github.io/dimensional/

## How to play
Create a room and share the 4-letter code or invite link. In the lobby, pick a
**game mode**, a **role**, and (optionally) your **dimension**.

**Keyboard:** A/D or ←/→ move · W/Space/↑ jump (fly up) · S/↓ fly down · F fire · Shift dash · E ping
**Phone:** joystick on the left (swipe up to jump), tap anywhere on the right to jump,
auto-fire shoots for you (toggle with AUTO). Choose controls on the main menu
(Automatic / Phone / Computer).

## Main menu
⚡ **Quick Play** drops you into a public room (or makes one others can find). 🎮 Solo (starts instantly, no forced bots), 👥 Create room,
🌍 Public rooms (browse and join; hosts can tick "Public room" in the lobby) or join with a 4-letter code.
👤 **Accounts**: sign up with a username and password to save your coins, stars and items in the cloud and
log in on any device.
👫 **Friends**: once you're logged in, add friends by username, accept requests, see who's 🟢 online and which
room they're in, hit ▶ Join to hop into their game, or 📨 Invite them to yours (also from the lobby).
🛠️ **Level Creator**: choose a 🧱 Platformer or a 🗺️ Top-down game. Build tab (tiles, coins, enemies, keys and doors,
signs…), Draw tab (paint floors, draw your own 12×12 pixel sprites), and a Scratch-style Logic tab
("WHEN I touch 🪙 Coin DO Play sound", "Every 10 seconds DO Spawn an enemy"…). Test it, then 🌐 publish it.
🌐 **Player levels**: play and like levels other players made.
🎲 **Mini games**: ☄️ Meteor Shower, 🎨 Paint the Floor, 🚦 Red Light Green Light, plus Tag, King of the Hill,
Coin Chaos, Last One Standing and Hot Potato.

## Look
The game starts in a **retro** style: hand-drawn pixel-art icons instead of emojis, pixel fonts, chunky boxes,
CRT scanlines and a chunky-pixel 3D world. Switch to **Modern** in ⚙️ Settings.
Easter egg: stay on the main menu for a while in retro style and the game slowly "upgrades" itself to modern
(turn it off in Settings). Start playing and it's retro again.

## 3D view
Every top-down game (roleplay maps, Color Blocks, Imposter, Tower Defense, Simon Says…) now shows in a **Roblox-style 3D
view**: blocky avatars that walk and swing their arms, block walls and trees with shadows, and a tilted camera that
follows you. Press **🎥** in a game (or ⚙️ Settings) to switch back to the classic flat 2D view.
The side-view games get a 3D camera too, swung round so you see the tops and sides of the blocks.

**Turn the camera** like Roblox: **← →** turn it round you, **↑ ↓** zoom in and out (or tilt, in side-view games), and
you move with **W A S D** — W always walks "up the screen", whichever way the camera faces. In side-view (2D) games
**🎥** switches between the normal flat view and the 3D camera (each kind of game remembers its own choice). Phones get **⟲ ⟳ ＋ －**
buttons. Switch it off in ⚙️ Settings and the arrow keys move you again.

The **🧊 3D games** tile on the menu (and the **All / 3D / 2D** tabs in Solo and the lobby) lists every 3D game.
The dimension card in the corner has a **✕** to shrink it to just your hearts and coins; **ℹ️** brings it back.

## More 3D games
Everyone plays as the same rounded-square guy as in the 2D levels.
- **🏎️ Car Race**: 3 laps round a 3D track against friends and bots. Boost pads, **Shift** nitro, **Space** to drift.
- **🌆 Open City** (GTA-style, cartoon and kid-friendly): walk or run around a 3D city and take any car with **E**,
  even one driving past. Do jobs for cash at the yellow circles (🚕 taxi, 🍕 pizza delivery, 🏁 street race at the police
  station). Hit the orange ramps for stunt-jump bonuses and buy a sports car ($400). The **foam blaster** (F / click)
  knocks people over. Crimes give you ★ wanted stars, and police cars chase you on the roads. If they catch you you're
  BUSTED, and getting hit by traffic too often means WASTED. Lose the cops or pay $100 at the 🎨 Spray Shop.
  Click to look around with the mouse.
- **🎯 Blaster Arena**: a first-person foam-blaster battle. Click to aim with the mouse, WASD to move, click to fire.
  First to 10 tags wins. Phones: drag on the right side to look.
- **🤖 Mars Rover**: a robotics mission. Drive a 6-wheeled rover over Mars, stop next to a glowing crystal and
  **hold E** so the robot arm drills it, then bring the samples to the lander. **Q** launches the scout drone, which
  shows samples on the radar. Watch the battery (solar panels charge it, the lander charges it fast) and the dust storms.
  The team goal is 10 samples.
- **🕊️ Free Flight**: the Flight island and plane with no rings and no clock.
- **🚪 Dimendoors** (DOORS, Dimensional style): a spooky first-person hotel of 50 numbered doors, alone or with friends.
  Walk into a door to open it. Search drawers (E) for gold and keys, because some doors are locked. The monsters:
  - **Rush**: the lights flicker, then it races through. Hide in a wardrobe (E)!
  - **Ambush**: like Rush, but it comes back again and again.
  - **Eyes**: a purple glow. Don't look at it.
  - **Screech**: "psst" in a dark room. Turn round and look at it.
  - **Seek**: from door 30 it chases you down the red halls. RUN!

  F turns on the flashlight. If you die you come back a few seconds later. Get through door 50 to escape.
- **⛏️ Survival** (Minecraft style): a blocky island world with pixel-art blocks.
  - Hold click (or F) to break blocks and right click (or G) to place them. 1–9 or the mouse wheel pick the hotbar
    slot, C opens your bag and crafting, and Q eats.
  - Punch trees for logs, then craft planks, a crafting table, sticks, wooden/stone/iron pickaxes and swords, a furnace,
    glass, bricks, lamps and wool. You need a pickaxe to collect stone, and a stone one for iron.
  - Hearts and hunger, fall damage, swimming, and a day and night cycle. At night zombies come out, and the sun burns
    them in the morning.
  - Friends build in the same world. It saves in your browser (or online, on a server).
  - **TNT** (4 sand + 2 coal): hit it and it hisses, then blows a crater. TNT nearby sets off a chain reaction.
  - **🪂 Glider** (4 wool + 3 sticks): keep it in your pack and hold Space while falling to soar forward. No fall damage while gliding.
  - **Beds** (3 wool + 3 planks): right-click one to set your spawn. At night, sleeping skips everyone to morning.
  - **Farming**: punching grass sometimes drops seeds. Plant them on dirt (right click) and wheat grows; 3 wheat
    make bread.
  - **Animals**: pigs, sheep and cows wander the grass. Pigs give porkchops, cows give steak, sheep give wool.
- **🧱 Creative**: the same world with every block, no monsters, and flying (double-tap Space; Space up, Shift down).
- **🗼 Tower of Doom** (Tower of Hell style): climb a random tower of 7 sections before the 6-minute clock runs out.
  Sections can be stairs, thin beams, lava floors, moving platforms, a spinning bar to jump or a ladder to climb.
  Touch red and you drop back to the start of that section. First to the top wins.
- **🌪️ Natural Disasters** (Natural Disaster Survival style): 6 rounds on an island town. Each round brings a flood,
  meteor shower, tornado, acid rain, earthquake or volcano. Climb the stairs to the roofs, hide under them, and stay
  away from the twister. Survive the most rounds to win.

## 🧱 Studio (make your own 3D game)
**Studio** on the main menu opens a Roblox-style game maker (`studio.html`):
- Add parts (block, ball, cylinder, wedge, spawn, coin, kill brick, bounce pad, goal, sign, light, teleporter,
  speed pad, checkpoint, NPC, door, water), then tap one and **drag**
  it with a tool: **✋ Move** (slide it along the floor), **↕️ Up/Down**, **⤢ Size** (sideways = wider, up/down = taller),
  **⬌ Depth**, or **🔄 Turn**. Keys 1–5 pick the tools on a computer. No typing numbers needed, though Properties still has them.
- **On a phone**: the 3D view fills the screen, the tools are a row along the bottom, and the floating
  **➕** (parts and toolbox), **▶** (play) and **⚙️** (edit) buttons open everything else. While playing, a joystick
  appears under your left thumb. Drag on the right side to look, and the big ⤴ button jumps.
- Set position, size, turn, color, solid, glow and tags in Properties. **Spin** and **Move** make a part turn or
  slide back and forth by itself (it carries you if you stand on it), with no code needed.
- What the new parts do on their own: teleporters with the same Tag link up, NPCs say their Text when you walk up,
  doors swing open when touched, checkpoints become your respawn point, speed pads make you fast for 5 seconds,
  lights glow, and you swim in water.
- The **🧰 Toolbox** has ready-made models: tree, house, tower, bridge, stairs, moving platform, moving lava,
  checkpoint, coin row, finish, lamp, car, jump pad, wall, lava floor and speed coil. The ones that need code add it
  to your Script for you.
- **🌐 Community models (UGC)**: Shift+click (or press **☑️ Many**) to pick some parts, then **🌐 Share as model**.
  Everyone can find it in **🌐 Community models** and insert it into their own game.
- Write the game's code in **📜 Script (JavaScript)**, e.g. `game.part("Door").onTouch(...)`, `game.onUpdate(dt => …)`,
  `game.score`, `game.win()`, `player.speed`. The list next to the editor shows everything you can use.
- Build the on-screen UI in **🖼️ UI (HTML)**.
- **▶ Play** runs it. Errors and `console.log` show in **🧾 Output**.
- Templates: Obby, Coin hunt, Rising lava.

- **2D or 3D**: every Studio game plays in full 3D *or* as a flat side-view platformer. Press **🎥** (or **V**) while
  playing to switch. In 2D you run left and right, and the world lines up side by side. Pick which view your game
  starts in under World → *Starts in*, or switch from a script with `game.view = "2d"`.

**🌐 Publish** (you need to be logged in with 👤 on the menu) puts your game online. Everyone can then play it from
**🎮 Studio games** on the main menu (also in 🌐 Player levels → 🧱 Studio games), like it, or **🧱 Remix** it to get
their own copy to edit. Publishing again updates your game. Games also save in your browser, and **Export** /
**Import** move them as a file. Games run in a sandboxed frame, so a script can't reach the main site or anyone's account.

## 🖥️ Servers
**Servers** on the main menu lists always-on rooms. A server keeps the same 4-letter code and runs the game mode its
owner picked. Join any time: in open-world modes (Survival, Creative, Open City, roleplay maps, Blaster Arena,
Dimendoors, Free Flight and more) you drop straight in, even mid-game. The game restarts by itself after a round
ends. Survival and Creative worlds are **saved online**, so what you build is still there next time.

Log in to make a server (up to 3 each). Pick a name, game mode, message of the day, and public (listed) or private
(join by code). Change or delete it later under ⭐ My servers.

## ✈️ Flight (3D)
Fly a Boeing-style airliner over a big 3D island, with friends (everyone flies their own plane) or alone. Take off from
Dimension Intl, fly through all **11 rings** (past mountains, radio towers, a city and two storms), then land on runway 09
at Cloud Peak Airfield. First to land and stop wins. Crash and you're back at your last ring.

Controls: **Shift/Space** throttle up, **X/Ctrl** throttle down, **← →** bank, **↑ ↓** climb/dive (**I** swaps to real pilot
style), **G** gear, **F** flaps, **↓ on the runway** brakes, **C** camera (chase, cockpit, far), **M** callouts on/off.
Phones: the stick flies, plus on-screen throttle, gear and flaps buttons.

**Cockpit callouts** (like a real Boeing GPWS): "TERRAIN, TERRAIN", "WHOOP WHOOP, PULL UP", "OBSTACLE, OBSTACLE",
"CAUTION TERRAIN", "SINK RATE", "DON'T SINK", "TOO LOW, GEAR", "TOO LOW, FLAPS", "TOO LOW, TERRAIN", "BANK ANGLE",
"AIRSPEED LOW", "STALL" (with the stick shaker), "OVERSPEED" (with the clacker), "WINDSHEAR", "GLIDESLOPE",
"TAKEOFF CONFIG", the takeoff calls ("eighty knots", "V one", "rotate", "positive rate") and the altitude calls on
landing ("one thousand… five hundred… minimums… fifty, forty, thirty, twenty, ten"). The voice is your browser's
built-in speech voice and the alarm sounds are made in the browser — they aren't recordings from a real Boeing.

## Game modes
- **Story Chapter 1 — The Spectre**: 13 co-op levels. An intro cutscene shows how the
  Spectre tore the friends apart. The final Spectre fight is tough (more health, faster attacks,
  homing missiles and grenades, and it gets ENRAGED) — and nobody comes to help.
- **Story Chapter 2 — The Unicoder**: everyone is in one world, but the Unicoder (a being
  made of Unicode letters) split the team: **Builders** can't walk but drag blocks;
  **Walkers** walk and push Builders. 5 levels: Lowercase, Gap Fill, Stack Overflow,
  Escape Sequence, The Unicoder.
- **Story Chapter 3 — The Floatter**: it floats between the worlds and hunts you with
  homing missiles and sound rockets (shockwave rings). Two hits and you're out. Hide under
  cover, shoot its missiles down, stun it — then take it down in the final fight.
  5 levels: Radio Silence, Static Bridges, Echo Chamber, Signal Jam, The Floatter.
- **Story Chapter 4 — Troppa**: a soldier the Spectre left behind, forced by its collar to hunt
  you (grenades and laser sweeps). Break the collar to free him, then fight the Spectre's return
  side by side. Cutscenes between levels and a final ending.
- **🪐 Across the Worlds**: a trip from Earth to a black hole: Earth → Venus (wind) → Mars (low
  gravity) → the Sun (solar flares) → Saturn → Jupiter (heavy gravity, storms) → Pluto (ice) → the
  Verse of Everything (dimensions reshuffle every 20s) → the Black Hole (it pulls you in; shoot its 4
  anchor stars to collapse it).
- **⏳ Through Time**: Dinosaur Age → Ancient Egypt → Medieval Castle → Pirate Seas → Wild West → Today →
  Future City → The End of Time → the Time Keeper boss.
- **🌊 Deep Sea**: Sunny Beach → Coral Reef → Shipwreck → Kelp Forest → The Abyss → the Kraken. Underwater you
  tap jump to swim.
- **🍌 Into the Bananaverse**: everyone is pulled into the top-down Bananaverse. Slash angry bananas through
  Banana Beach, Peel Village, the Jungle Maze and the Pirate Docks, then beat **Captain Peel** (banana boomerangs,
  stomps and minions).
- **💀 Parallel World**: the opposite game — your goal is to DIE, and the world won't let you. Spikes bounce you,
  pits and lasers save you, crawlers are guardian angels that shove you away, and the 💀 Doom Gate drifts, shuts
  and blows a "life wind". Every human player has to get into the gate (bots are optional).
- **🧗 Obby (2D)**: a Roblox-style obstacle course race in 3 courses (Easy Obby, Tower of Jumps, Mega Obby): kill bricks,
  moving platforms, jump pads, tiny pillars and 🚩 checkpoint flags. First to the end wins each round.
- **🌋 The Floor is Lava (2D)**: the floor keeps melting into lava — stay on the blocks. A point per second you survive.
- **🔺 Geometry Beats**: 8 more rhythm-dash levels (Neon Steps → Final Frequency, 120 to 176 BPM) with new tall blocks to
  land on. No help gates, so they work solo. Beat all 8 for +50 🪙.
- **Story Chapter 5 — The Conductor**: a Geometry Dash–style rhythm chapter that is still co-op. You run on your own
  and only jump; every obstacle sits on the beat of the song (a kick drum on every beat) and everyone gets the same
  snappy physics. One hit and you crash. 🟡 **Help orbs**: jump inside one to fly over a gap — it also opens a **gate in
  your friend's lane**. 💚 **Revive orbs**: crashed friends come back when someone still running flies through one.
  If everybody crashes, the song restarts instantly. % progress bar, attempt counter and best %. 5 songs from 128 to
  170 BPM: First Beat, Off the Beat, Call and Response, Double Time, The Conductor's Symphony.
- **Story Chapter 6 — The Ship**: a rift opens on a slave ship crossing the Atlantic. The cutscenes explain the real
  history plainly (the transatlantic slave trade, and resistance such as the 1839 Amistad revolt). In 5 co-op levels
  (The Coast, The Cargo Hold, Below Deck, The Storm, Turn the Ship Around) you unlock every captive in both lanes —
  the way off the ship stays locked until everyone is free. Guards can only be knocked out for a few seconds.
- **🎯 Who is the sniper god?**: free-for-all sniping across two floating islands. Aim with the mouse (aim assist on
  phones), click or F to fire, one hit = out. Most kills in 2 minutes is crowned the Sniper God.
- **🛹 Parkour**: skateboard races across procedurally built rooftops with low gravity. Rolling momentum, grind rails,
  boost pads, launch ramps, personal checkpoint flags, and air tricks (hold DOWN in the air).
- **🎨 Color Blocks** (top-down): a color is called — run onto a tile of that color before the timer ends, every
  other tile drops. It gets faster each round. Last one standing wins.
- **🦎 Crazy Chameleon** (top-down): camouflage hide-and-seek. Hiders stand still to turn the color of the floor; the
  seeker tags them (F). Every 20 seconds the colors go CRAZY and hiders must move to blend in again.
- **🔥 99 Nights in the Dimensions** (top-down): survive in a dark forest. Chop trees (F) and feed the campfire (E);
  its light keeps the monsters away (Shadow Wolves, Frost Wraiths, the Rift Stag, Banana Brutes). Roles: Lumberjack,
  Fighter, Medic, Scout. Eat berries, rescue the 4 lost kids, come back at dawn if you fall. Reach night 99 to win.
- **🔪 Imposter** (top-down, Among Us style, 4+ players — bots fill in): crewmates hold E at their task markers;
  the imposter takes people out with F. Report a body (E) or hit the emergency button to meet, chat, and vote someone
  off. Crew wins by finishing tasks or ejecting the imposter; the imposter wins when it's 1 vs 1.
- **🚑 Emergency Response** (top-down, Dead Rails style): ride a rescue train 3.4 km across a zombie wasteland to the
  Safe Zone. Shovel coal into the engine (E), refill the coal car from town coal piles while the train waits at
  stations, bring survivors aboard, and shoot zombies (F) — especially at night.
- **🧟 Zombie Infection**, **🚩 Capture the Flag**, **🏍️ Light Trails** (Tron light cycles), **💣 Blast Arena**
  (Bomberman style with power-ups) and **⚽ Dimension Soccer** — all top-down, all playable with bots.
- **🏫 School (roleplay)**: Student, Teacher, Principal, Janitor, Nurse, Lunch Cook, Coach (whistle, "run a lap!"), Librarian (lends books, shushes) or Bus Driver. The bell moves everyone through
  the day (Homeroom, Math, Science, Lunch, Recess, PE, Reading, Art). Teachers write on the chalkboard (`/board`) and give

- **🏙️ City (roleplay)**: jobs include Citizen, Police, Robber, Doctor, Firefighter, Chef, Shopkeeper, Mayor, Mail Carrier (deliver mail for $5), Street Musician (play for tips) and Mechanic (fix cars for $10). Earn money every payday from your job, shop (candy to crowns), buy a house for $100 and lock it,
  drive cars (E near one), play the arcade. Robbers rob the bank vault or pickpocket — police arrest wanted robbers and
  lock them in jail. Firefighters put out fires that break out around town, the mayor speaks from City Hall, and day

- **🛸 Area 51 (roleplay)**: pick a job (Guard, Scientist, Alien, Janitor, Agent, Tourist, Base Medic, UFO Pilot, Cafeteria Cook) and play it out. Keycard

  /wave, /sit, /announce, /job.
- **🏰 Tower Defense**: build and upgrade Arrow, Cannon, Frost and Laser towers with shared coins. 20 waves, bosses.
- **🧪 Training Grounds**: pads to try every role and
  every dimension, respawning dummies and all the gadgets.
- **🎮 Solo**: pick any mode from the menu and play alone. Bots join in automatically when a mode

- **⏱️ Time Trial**: all 6 race maps alone against the clock, with medals and your best time.

**Chapter styles:** Chapter 3 is a sky factory, Chapter 4 a war zone, party games are neon arcade.

**📅 Daily Challenge:** one level + one twist per day (low gravity, turbo, lights out, crawler swarm,

**Rewards:** earn 🪙 coins from levels (⭐ stars for finishing, no deaths and beating par time), hidden
coins, crawlers, party wins and dailies. Spend them in the 🛍️ Shop on hats, trails, shot colors and

## Tech
- Static HTML/JS with **Three.js** for 3D, hosted on **GitHub Pages** (no build step).
- **Supabase Realtime** (broadcast + presence) for rooms. The first player in the room
  hosts gates, waves and the boss, and sends a full snapshot every 2 seconds so
  dropped messages and reconnects catch up. Rooms rejoin automatically if the connection drops.
  Players who join mid-level watch until the next level.
- **Supabase Postgres** table `dim_clears` for leaderboards (`supabase/schema.sql`).

To use a different Supabase project, run `supabase/schema.sql` in its SQL editor
and put its URL and publishable key in `config.js`.

## Adding levels
Levels are small functions in `js/levels.js` that place shared tiles and objects
bound to a dimension (plates, bridges, doors, pads, rifts, keys, lasers, crystals,
gravity fields, crawlers). Add one to a mode's list in `MODES` and it shows up in the game.
