DIMENSIONAL

2–20 players. Every one of you in a different dimension. Work together anyway.

How to play
  • A/D or ←/→ move · W/Space/↑ jump · F fire · E ping
  • On a phone: drag on the left side to move, tap the buttons on the right. Play sideways! You can switch controls below.
  • Floating dimensions fly with up/down (W/S). 2Dverse can dash with Shift.
  • Pick a role and a dimension in the lobby, or leave it on Auto.
  • No friends online? The host can add 🤖 bots that hold plates, shoot and race.
  • Each player is in their own dimension with its own physics — grassy, upside-down, icy, low-gravity, mirrored, dark…
  • You can see everyone as ghosts, but you only touch things from your dimension.
  • Plates, pads, rifts and keystones link dimensions together. Nobody can win alone.
